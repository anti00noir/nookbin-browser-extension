NookSuite GitHub

Small tools that stay out of your way.

NookSuite is a set of local-first browser tools. Your files and notes stay on your own machine, permissions stay minimal, and nothing phones home. Extensions today; native apps tomorrow.

The tools

Principles

  • Local-first Your files stay on your machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • No accounts Install and use. No sign-ups, no sync walls.
  • No telemetry The tools do not collect, report, or phone home.
  • Minimal permissions Each tool asks for only what its feature needs.

Roadmap

  1. Now NookBin — a floating file panel for Chrome.
  2. Next Harden NookBin, and shape NookEdit and NookNotes.
  3. Later Native ports of each tool, sharing one core.
